How to play

You have 90 seconds of business hours. Make as many sales as you can!

Basics

  1. 1Check the order

    The speech bubble above each customer shows the sushi they want. The bar under it shows how long they'll wait. It goes from green to yellow to red.

    Customers at the front counter sit with their backs to you, so their orders appear inside the belt.

  2. 2Tap a sushi button

    Tap a sushi button at the bottom and the chef shouts "Coming up!". The plate goes onto the belt right in front of a customer who wants that sushi.

    If several customers want the same sushi, the one who has waited longest gets it first.

  3. 3Deliver it on the belt

    Customers can only grab the plate that passes directly in front of them. If it's the sushi they want, they take it and eat it.

    If a plate is already in front of the customer, yours goes into the next empty spot behind it. On a crowded belt, delivery takes longer.

  4. 4Wrong plates keep going

    If you send out sushi nobody ordered, the plate just keeps going around the belt. A customer who orders it later may still pick it up.

    But a plate that goes around twice without being taken is thrown away (−¥20).

Rules

Business hours90 seconds. As time passes, the belt speeds up and more customers arrive.
Customers5 seats at the back counter and 5 at the front, up to 10 customers. After eating, they order again or leave.
Angry customersWhen the wait bar runs out, they leave in a huff ("I'm leaving!"). The 😠 marks at the top right fill up.
ClosingAnger 5 customers and the shop closes right away (game over).
Lane is fullIf there's no empty spot on the belt, you can't place a plate. Don't let wrong plates pile up.

How sales add up

Sushi priceThe price of each plate a customer takes is added to your sales (¥120–¥300).
Speed bonusUp to +¥100, depending on how much of the wait bar is left.
ComboServe in a row and each plate from the 2nd combo on earns an extra +¥20 more. A wrong plate or an angry customer resets it.
WasteA plate that goes around twice without being taken: −¥20.
RankingMake the top 10 and you can save your name. See the records on the Ranking page (saved in this browser).
TitlesApprentice → Skilled Cook (¥2,500+) → Sushi Chef (¥6,000+) → Master Chef (¥10,000+) → Legendary Master (¥15,000+)

Controls

Phones and tablets: tap the sushi buttons. Hold your phone upright for a larger, portrait layout.
Computers: click, or use the keyboard.

Tips

Red bars first

When several customers want the same sushi, the plate automatically goes to whoever has waited longest. When in doubt, serve the red-bar customers first.

Read the belt

The top row moves left to right and the bottom row moves right to left. A jam in front of a customer delays your plate.

Don't button-mash

Wrong plates block the belt and break your combo. Double-check the bubble before you tap.

Pricey orders are chances

Serve Sea Urchin (¥300) and Salmon Roe (¥250) quickly to keep your combo going.
