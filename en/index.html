Why it's fun

Just tap

Tap a sushi button and the plate is delivered in front of the customer who wants it. Plays on phones and computers.

A real conveyor belt

If a plate is already in front of the customer, yours goes into the next empty spot behind it. Wrong plates keep going round and round.
